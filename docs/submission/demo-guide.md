## A concise, repeatable story

**Question:** Can a viewer understand an important passage without trusting an unexplained AI verdict?

1. **Introduce the product.** Show the landing page and the five-stage pipeline. State that synthetic scenes are generated, not league tracking.
2. **Enter the workspace.** Explicitly choose/start a seeded scenario. Show the recipe/source label and match clock.
3. **Play the passage.** Compare source/pitch/split views as available. Explain that preparation and event transport are not the playback clock.
4. **Open a moment.** Inspect its window, measurements, entity attribution, and evidence before requesting a story.
5. **Change perspective.** Compare Fan, Analyst, and Player. Show insufficient player evidence honestly; do not manufacture involvement.
6. **Change language.** Demonstrate retained numbers/evidence. State whether the displayed passage is model or deterministic text.
7. **Listen deliberately.** Show ordered lead/analyst turns, actual provider labels, pause/seek invalidation, and that manual deep Listen pauses replay without auto-restarting it.
8. **Close with trust.** Show a recap and explain what is unknown/withheld. Link the evidence map and cloud release gates.

## If demonstrating video

Use only footage with documented analysis/playback/publication rights. Supply a licensed local detector and real surveyed camera landmarks where physical geometry is shown. Unknown score, missing ball, invalidated calibration, and truncated analyzed duration should remain visible.

Do not download protected material, use private live-preview uploads, or publish footage merely because it was previously tracked in Git.

## If a provider is unavailable

Keep the labelled deterministic text/browser fallback visible and describe exactly what happened. Do not call a fallback a successful Foundry/Speech or Framework demonstration. Real-provider diagnostics are separate, explicitly approved billable checks.

## Presenter checklist

- Identify the release/recipe/seed and whether the environment is local or verified staging.
- Prepare a clean browser session without credentials or private data visible.
- Verify configured voices and actual provider status before promising audio.
- Capture only real synthetic scenes; no fabricated screenshots, metrics, or badges.
- Do not claim Microsoft sign-in, guest trials, cloud scaling, or Agent Framework execution before their integrated evidence is available.
- Keep [setup commands](../development/getting-started.md) separate from the product story.
